Evaluation

Measured on the sample dataset via /api/evaluation. No invented metrics.

Methodology

  • Retrieval: TF-IDF with Unicode-safe tokenization (Devanagari-aware), scored with cosine similarity. 47 held-out queries; MRR and hit-rate@5 reported.
  • Safety: 11 validator checks covering dosage refusal, disclaimer presence, and PII handling — all passing.
  • Grounding: 6 hallucination probes verify answers stay within retrieved records; low-confidence queries get clarifying questions, not guesses.
  • Splits: leakage-controlled 201/47/47 split with no ID overlap and 0.0% near-duplicates.
  • Baselines: retriever and model comparisons (BM25 vs embeddings, base vs adapted) live in the companion ML pipeline's experiments/ directory.
These figures describe the sample-data pipeline. Re-run evaluation after expanding the knowledge base or swapping in a trained adapter.
